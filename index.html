How this disaster works

  • Four racers begin at the food stand and race toward the maze exit.
  • You get a bird's-eye look at the full labyrinth before the camera dives down into the maze.
  • Shift uses stamina for a normal sprint. During a bowel emergency, sprinting also raises your poop risk.
  • When the bowel alarm hits, the EXIT stops mattering. Two one-use toilets become the only safe havens.
  • You may open the full map only 5 times. Every peek adds bowel pressure.
  • The closer you are to disaster, the slower and more ridiculous your walk becomes.
  • The first two racers to claim the two toilets survive. The other two immediately lose control of the situation.
